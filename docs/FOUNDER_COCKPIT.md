# Founder Cockpit — Luxe Control Surface

The cockpit is not a chat window. It is a decision surface.

## First viewport

`LIVE SYSTEMS` — healthy services and deployment state

`APPROVAL QUEUE` — actions requiring founder decision

`ACTIVE RUNS` — currently executing chains

`BLOCKERS` — anything preventing verified progress

`SPEND` — current and projected cost against policy

`EVIDENCE` — latest proof attached to material actions

`REGIONS` — EU / UK / US operating status

`ROBOTICS` — physical systems and safety state

## Founder microcopy

- `Clear.` — system is healthy and verified.
- `Needs you.` — a bounded decision is waiting.
- `Moving.` — an approved execution chain is active.
- `Blocked.` — execution stopped; evidence explains why.
- `Proven.` — the result has verification attached.
- `Contained.` — safety or policy isolation is active.

## Decision card

Every approval shows:

`ACTION | WHY | COST | RISK | REGION | ROLLBACK | EVIDENCE | APPROVE / REJECT`

No hidden secondary action.

## Visual rules

- one dominant action per screen
- generous whitespace
- high information density without visual noise
- status first, explanation second
- evidence one tap away
- no decorative AI claims
- no fake live indicators
- no success state without verification
