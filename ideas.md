# Design Direction — Bulgarian GCE AI Strategy

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Editorial Intelligence Atlas
Very Brief Intro: A premium research-publication interface where market evidence is organized like an atlas: warm paper, ink-black typography, saffron signals, and precise visual annotations. It makes complex strategic material feel authoritative, legible, and worth returning to.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Civic Signal Room
Very Brief Intro: A light, civic-tech dashboard language built around clean white space, institutional blue, and data-first cards. It feels transparent and practical, optimized for workshops and policy conversations.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Agent Constellation
Very Brief Intro: A dark, high-contrast systems map with copper highlights, orbiting nodes, and a subtle AI-network atmosphere. It creates energy and ambition for a future-facing pitch, but risks over-indexing on tech spectacle.
Probability: 0.08

## Selected Approach: Editorial Intelligence Atlas

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorialism with Swiss information design and modernist research-report cues, softened by Bulgarian craft references: parchment warmth, red-orange saffron markers, and restrained blue-green data tones.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before spectacle:** every visual cue should help the reader scan, compare, or decide.
2. **Asymmetry with purpose:** use a left rail, offset columns, and editorial notes rather than a centered SaaS landing-page stack.
3. **Human-readable strategy:** translate frameworks into plain-language decisions, not consultant jargon.
4. **Local signal, global scale:** use Bulgarian visual warmth and language sensitivity while keeping the structure globally legible.

### Color Philosophy
Deep ink (#10212B) anchors trust and seriousness. Warm paper (#F4EFE6) keeps long-form analysis approachable. A signature saffron (#E06B3C) marks opportunity, conviction, and action without looking like generic tech orange. Muted teal (#4E8078) communicates systems, evidence, and balance. Dusty rose (#C78573) is reserved for risk and strategic tension. The palette should feel like a well-used research folio, not a neon product dashboard.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent editorial rail introduces sections and source context while the main canvas alternates between wide narrative bands and offset analytical modules. Charts sit beside interpretation rather than below it. Dense content is broken with pull quotes, score chips, source notes, and horizontal rules. On mobile, the rail collapses into a compact chapter bar and the reading order remains narrative-first.

### Signature Elements
- Saffron vertical signal bars beside decisive insights.
- Small-caps chapter labels with issue/date metadata.
- Ink-paper panels with thin rules, asymmetric corner treatments, and index-like numerals.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a research dossier: active filters, tabs, and accordions reveal context without losing the reader's place. Hover states expose why a score exists. Buttons use direct verbs such as “Compare lenses” and “Read the caveat,” never vague conversion language.

### Animation
Use short 180–260ms transitions with a strong ease-out. Animate chart bars and chapter reveals by opacity and translateY only; stagger related elements by 40ms. Let selected tabs slide a saffron indicator into place. Avoid floating, bouncing, or perpetual motion. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

### Typography System
Display: **DM Serif Display**, used for strategic thesis statements and chapter headlines. Body/UI: **Manrope**, used for prose, labels, controls, and data. Small caps use Manrope at 0.72rem with 0.16em tracking. Headlines are sentence case, not all caps. Numeric scores use Manrope 700 with tabular figures.

### Brand Essence
A decision-grade lens on Bulgarian-language AI opportunity for founders, educators, and ecosystem builders—different because it joins market evidence with a human, regional point of view.
Personality: **discerning, grounded, catalytic**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, specific, and slightly editorial. CTAs are practical and action-led. Microcopy names uncertainty instead of hiding it.
Example lines: “The opportunity is not ‘more AI’; it is a trusted layer between language, workflow, and action.” “Start with the wedge you can prove in 90 days.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact mark built from three offset vertical saffron strokes entering a dark circular field, suggesting Bulgarian Cyrillic signal, an agent stack, and an atlas coordinate. The wordmark is set separately in DM Serif Display with a clipped terminal on the final letter; it should never be treated as a default typed logo.

### Signature Brand Color
**Saffron Signal — #E06B3C**. It is warm enough to feel human and Bulgarian, strong enough to call attention to action, and distinctive against ink and paper.

## Content and evidence guardrails

This is a strategic analysis, not an endorsement or valuation. Publicly verifiable information will be cited inline. When a named entity has limited public evidence, the page will label the item as an informed assumption, a directional hypothesis, or an evidence gap rather than presenting it as fact. The framework will combine PESTEL, Five Forces, SWOT, a business-model lens, a 7Ps-style go-to-market view, and a 90-day strategic roadmap.
